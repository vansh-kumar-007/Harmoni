Interactive Multi-Theme Architecture: Harmoni is a multi-theme music platform. Every signed-in user chooses their preferred theme independently. Use the Theme Selector in the header or in View 2 to switch between all five themes in real-time. Notice how the sidebar, hero cards, vinyl artwork, track listings, and docked music player immediately transform their surfaces, accents, and contrast.

Root-Level <html data-theme="..."> Architecture: Themes are applied directly on the root <html> element to mirror the production contract where Next.js 16 server-renders the user's preference with zero flash of unstyled theme (zero-FOUC).
